A Telegram sidecar could stay alive but stop receiving messages after a transient error. The poll round-trip had no overall deadline: `urllib`'s timeout bounds one socket operation, not the whole response, so a 502 whose body never terminated could park the worker thread forever, with no log line and no process exit for the supervisor to notice.
`produce()` now wraps the poll in a 50 s deadline, distinguishes a normal long-poll timeout from a wedged thread, and escalates three consecutive wedges to a producer crash so the supervisor restarts the sidecar; repeated timeouts and long gaps without a successful poll are logged at WARN, rate-limited.
